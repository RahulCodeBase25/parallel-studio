## The brief

Northstar's dispatchers tracked 2,000 trucks across seven tools, three monitors and a lot of phone calls. When a delivery ran late, finding out why took twenty minutes.

## What we did

We sat with dispatchers in Dallas and Memphis for a week of night shifts. Every design decision came from those shifts: a single live map, exceptions ranked by how much they will cost, and a timeline for each load that shows the whole story of a delay at a glance.

Colour is used only for problems. A calm screen means everything is on time.

## What changed

The time to resolve a late delivery dropped by 42%. Northstar retired six internal tools.
