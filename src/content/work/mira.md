## The brief

Most sleep apps keep you on your phone: streaks, badges, one more story. Mira wanted the opposite, an app that helps you put the phone down.

## What we did

We designed the app around the minutes before sleep. The screen dims with you, text gets larger and warmer, and after you press play the interface fades to nothing. There are no streaks. In the morning there's one gentle question instead of a chart.

The motion system is slow on purpose: nothing moves faster than a breath.

## What changed

Users slept 19 minutes longer on average in their first month, and kept 62% of them after 30 days.
