## The brief

Kiln sells handmade ceramics from small studios. Their old site looked like every other marketplace: a grid of products on white, which made a $240 hand-thrown vase look like a $9 mug.

## What we did

We gave every maker a studio page with process photos, a short story and their glaze notes, and made those pages the heart of the site. Product pages show the piece next to the hands that made it. A "made to order" flow lets buyers commission a set and follow it through the kiln.

The identity is built from the materials: raw clay tones, a typeface with the slight wobble of a hand-cut stamp, and photography shot in the makers' own spaces.

## What changed

Buyers who visited a studio page spent more than twice as much. Kiln now onboards makers through the same studio-page builder we designed.
