## The brief

Ledger's customers are freelancers: photographers, nurses on agency shifts, delivery riders. Their pay arrives in lumps, on no schedule, and every budgeting app they had tried assumed a salary on the 1st.

## What we did

We started with three weeks of interviews and a shoebox of real bank statements (with permission). The pattern was clear: people didn't want a budget, they wanted to know **what they could safely spend today**. So the home screen became one number, recalculated every time money comes in or goes out, with tax and rent already set aside.

We built the app on a small design system of 40 components, so Ledger's two in-house engineers could ship new screens without waiting on design.

## What changed

The "safe to spend" number tested so well that Ledger moved it into a widget and onto the lock screen. Six months after launch, users were 31% more likely to keep a tax pot topped up.
